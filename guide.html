Before you book

Your matchday, step by step.

Choose a fixture, select an available stadium section, link your Fan ID, and review the full amount before payment.

Browse matches
01

Choose the fixture

Open the match you plan to attend from the home page. Confirm the teams, date, kick-off time, and venue before continuing. Each match has its own ticket page.

02

Pick a section on the stadium map

Select an available section to see its category and current price. The section price is added to your cart automatically. Sections marked unavailable cannot be selected.

03

Check the currency and total

Prices are entered in Kenyan shillings (KES). The display selector can show an estimate in USD, KES, TZS, or UGX using fixed planning rates. Currency conversion changes the display only; it is not a live exchange quote.

04

Link the ticket to a Fan ID

Enter the Fan ID requested during checkout and review the ticket-holder details carefully. The current ticketing rules limit each Fan ID to one ticket per match and require personalized digital tickets.

05

Review payment and the hold timer

Review the seat, match, service levy, and final total before proceeding. A selected seat is held for up to 10 minutes during checkout; an expired hold may release the section.

06

Keep your digital ticket secure

Use only the official ticketing channel and follow the entry instructions issued with your confirmed ticket. Bring the required identification and be ready for the verification checks stated by the event organizer.

Travel and entry

A match ticket is not, by itself, a visa.

The project rules explicitly state that a ticket alone does not grant entry across a border. Any PAMOJA visa-waiver or regional travel arrangement depends on current official approval and its published conditions. Check the immigration authority for every country you plan to visit before booking travel.

Before checkout

  • Choose the correct match and host venue.
  • Check category, availability, fees, and total.
  • Use a unique Fan ID for the ticket holder.
  • Allow enough time to complete the checkout hold.

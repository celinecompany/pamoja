Tournament Clusters

Kenya Hub • Nairobi

Talanta Sports Stadium (Nairobi) • 60,000 Capacity.
The purpose-built modern football cathedral hosting the official Opening Ceremony and Match.

Moi International Sports Centre (Kasarani) • 60,000 Capacity.
Fully refurbished with Category 4 CAF standards, VIP suites, and rapid transport links.

Nyayo National Stadium • 30,000 Capacity.
Historic Nairobi venue hosting group-stage fixtures.

Tanzania Hub • Dar, Arusha & Zanzibar

Benjamin Mkapa Stadium (Dar es Salaam) • 60,000 Capacity.
Host of the Grand Final and closing coronation with ocean-breeze atmosphere.

Samia Suluhu Hassan Stadium (Arusha) • 30,000 Capacity.
Brand new northern tourist circuit hub hosting knockout fixtures.

Amaan Complex (Zanzibar) • 15,000 Capacity.
Vibrant island stadium hosting group-stage matches.

Uganda Hub • Kampala & Hoima

Mandela National Stadium (Namboole, Kampala) • 45,000 Capacity.
Modernized national home of the Uganda Cranes hosting Groups C and D.

Hoima City Stadium • 20,000 Capacity.
New state-of-the-art arena hosting group stages and semi-final block.

Akii-Bua Olympic Stadium (Lira) • 20,000 Capacity.
Northern Uganda tournament center.

Matchday Security, Gates & Transit Integration

All tournament venues feature biometric & encrypted QR digital turnstiles, designated fan park shuttles, accessibility ramps for fans with disabilities, and dedicated hospitality pavilions. Ticket holders should arrive 2 hours prior to kickoff with their verified digital ticket passes.

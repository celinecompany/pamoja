AFCON 2027 Qualifiers

CAF Official ↗

A total of 54 CAF member associations entered qualification for 28 spots in the final tournament. Kenya, Tanzania and Uganda qualify automatically as co-hosts. The group stage draw took place on 13 January 2026 — qualifying runs through to November 2026.

Tanzania are already qualified — as co-hosts alongside Kenya and Uganda , the Taifa Stars have an automatic berth. The remaining 25 spots are decided through qualifying.

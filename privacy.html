Data Protection & Security

Privacy & Data Governance Notice.

How CAF and the Local Organizing Committees of Kenya, Tanzania, and Uganda safeguard your personal information, Fan ID verification, and transaction records.

1. Information We Collect

When purchasing tickets or registering a YALLA Fan ID, we collect your full legal name, email address, mobile phone number, payment details (processed via encrypted payment gateways), and government-issued ID for stadium access security.

2. Purpose of Data Processing

Your information is processed strictly for: issuing verified digital ticket QR passes, preventing illegal ticket scalping and fraudulent transfers, managing venue capacity limits, dispatching matchday safety notifications, and verifying identity at venue turnstiles.

3. Data Protection & Security Standards

All transactional data and identity credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-256 and transmitted over TLS 1.3. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data is shared exclusively with certified venue access controllers and payment processors.

4. Your Rights & Data Requests

You have the right to access, rectify, or request the deletion of your personal account data upon tournament conclusion, subject to regulatory compliance. To submit a data request, please contact Fan Support.

Ticketing Terms & Conditions.

Official terms governing match ticket purchases, allocation, venue admission, and supporter code of conduct for AFCON PAMOJA 2027.

1. Ticket Validity & Authenticity

Each issued match ticket constitutes a personal revocable license. Tickets are valid strictly for the match, seat block, stadium venue, and designated gate specified. Tickets purchased from unauthorized secondary platforms or touts are null and void.

2. Stadium Entry & YALLA ID

Ticket holders must follow the match-specific identity and entry checks published by the organizer. This prototype does not issue production QR passes or verify identity. A match ticket alone is not a visa, entry permit, or guarantee of border admission; confirm travel requirements with official immigration authorities.

3. Transfers & Resale Policy

Tickets are personalized and non-transferable under the current project rules. Do not rely on this prototype for ticket transfers or resale; follow the official organizer's published ticket-holder policy.

4. Safety, Prohibited Items & Code of Conduct

Pyrotechnics, flares, bottles, cans, laser pointers, and political banners are strictly prohibited. CAF and venue security reserve the right to inspect bags and refuse entry to individuals exhibiting disruptive behavior.
